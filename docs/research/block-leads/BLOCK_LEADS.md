## Main conflicts

- Overview index and block vignettes disagree on 13 numbers: 13, 19, 20, 21, 29, 30, 31, 32, 33, 37, 38, 55, 56 (see per-lead conflicts).
- Administrative blocks 29-32 look shifted by one between sources (vignette: 29 Pro-Chancellor, 30 Chancellery, 31 Admissions, 32 Vice-Chancellor; index: 29 Administrative, 30 Prochancellor, 31 Chancellor/Administrative, 32 Admission).
- Engineering numbering differs: vignette 55 Mechanical / 56 Civil; index 56 Mechanical / 38 Civil (vignette 38 = Computer Applications).
- Girls hostels: vignette has 9-12 (GH 1-4) and 22,23 (GH 5,6); index has 9-12 and 21A,B with no 22/23.
- Boys hostels: index '45,48,49,50,51,53,52' includes 52 (a pin on the cricket ground) and omits 54; Boys Hostel 4 is never assigned.
- Overview omits pins 5, 16, 19, 22, 23, 31, 52, 55 that appear on vignettes/scenes.
- slide-02.png and slide-06.png are byte-identical.
- Index `BUSINESS 13` vs vignette `BLOCK-13 DIVISION OF STUDENT WELFARE` and `BLOCK-14 MITTAL SCHOOL OF BUSINESS` (14 is not in the index).
- Index `RESEARCH 37` vs vignette `BLOCK-37: CENTRAL LIBRARY`.
- Index `LAW 20` vs vignette `BLOCK-21 SCHOOL OF LAW`; index `GIRLS HOSTEL … 21A,B` vs block 21 as law. Overview pin 21 sits on a residential-looking cluster separate from pin 20, while the vignette draws 19/20/21 as one connected building.
- Index residential `51,53,52` vs vignettes 53,54 = `BOYS HOSTEL - 5, 6`; the only pin 52 is on the cricket ground.

## Next steps for the owner

1. Treat each lead as a question to confirm against an official LPU source (signage, official map, prospectus) before any number or name touches the dataset.
2. Where vignette and index disagree, neither wins by default; record both until confirmed.
3. `shapeNotes` in the JSON describe the drawing's massing to help a human shortlist candidate OSM footprints; a shape match alone is not evidence of identity.
